Finding us

  1. From Tillamook, head west on Hwy 131, about 2 miles past the hospital.
  2. Look for the purple Minnesota Vikings bus shack and the eggs-for-sale sign on the right.
  3. Turn up the cement driveway (it turns to gravel) and watch for the camping sign.
  4. Coming from the south on Fraiser Rd? Turn right onto Hwy 131 — we're under a quarter mile on your left.
Google Maps has sent some campers the wrong way. Follow these landmarks — the exact address arrives after booking.

Good to know

  • Clean porta-potties and potable water near every site. No showers or hookups.
  • Campfires allowed. Gather dead wood only — no cutting live trees, no chainsaws. Firewood bundles are sold nearby for about $5.
  • Sites #3 and #4-B are on dirt: 4WD / AWD preferred, and no trailers on #4-B.
  • You're in dairy country — expect the odd moo from farms across the river, and some road noise. Earplugs help light sleepers.
  • Campers report having cell service on the property.
